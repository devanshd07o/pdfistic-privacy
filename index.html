Privacy Policy

Last updated: June 2026

PDFistic is a desktop PDF reader built with Electron. We are committed to protecting your privacy. This policy explains what data we collect, how we use it, and your rights.

1. Data We Collect

PDFistic is primarily a local, offline application. By default, no personal data is sent to external servers.

  • Recent Files List — Stored locally on your device only.
  • App Preferences — Theme, zoom, font settings. Stored locally.
  • API Keys — If you add AI provider keys, they are stored encrypted on your device using Windows DPAPI. Never shared with PDFistic servers.

2. Google Sign-In (Optional)

If you choose to sign in with Google:

  • We request your basic Google profile (name, email, profile picture) via OAuth 2.0.
  • Your credentials are stored encrypted on your local device only — never on a PDFistic server.
  • We do not access your Google Drive, Gmail, or any other Google services.
  • You can sign out at any time from Account settings.

Google's privacy policy: policies.google.com/privacy

3. AI Features (Optional)

If you use AI Chat features:

  • Text from the current PDF page may be sent to your chosen AI provider (Google Gemini, Groq, etc.).
  • These requests go directly from your device to the AI provider — PDFistic does not proxy or store them.
  • AI features require your own API key, stored only on your device.

4. Data We Do NOT Collect

  • We do not collect usage analytics or telemetry.
  • We do not collect crash reports automatically.
  • We do not have access to the content of your PDF files.
  • We do not sell, share, or transmit any personal data to third parties.
  • We do not use advertising SDKs or tracking libraries.

5. Local Storage

All app data is stored locally in: %APPDATA%\pdfistic

You can delete this folder at any time to completely wipe all app data.

6. Children's Privacy

PDFistic is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal data from children.

7. Changes to This Policy

We may update this privacy policy from time to time. Changes will be reflected on this page with an updated date.
